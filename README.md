# 📊 Online Store Performance Analysis

> An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends.

---

## 📌 Overview

**Online Store Performance Analysis** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to provide a centralized view of e-commerce business performance.

The dashboard analyzes key business metrics such as **total sales, total profit, orders, customers, quantity sold, and profit margin**.

It also provides visual insights into sales trends, product performance, customer segments, product categories, and regional profitability through interactive charts and slicers.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 💰 **Sales Analysis** — Monitor total sales and analyze sales trends over time.
- 📈 **Profit Analysis** — Track total profit and profit margin.
- 🛒 **Order Analysis** — Monitor total orders and quantity sold.
- 👥 **Customer Analysis** — Analyze sales across different customer segments.
- 📦 **Product Performance** — Identify products contributing to overall sales.
- 🗂️ **Category Analysis** — Compare sales performance across product categories.
- 🌎 **Regional Analysis** — Compare profitability across different regions.
- 🎯 **Interactive Slicers** — Filter dashboard results by order date, region, segment, and category.
- 📊 **KPI Cards** — Display key business metrics at a glance.
- 🔄 **Dynamic Visualizations** — Charts and metrics update based on selected filters.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **DAX** — Used for calculated measures and business metrics.
- **Power Query** — Used for data preparation and transformation.
- **Data Visualization** — Interactive charts, KPI cards, and slicers.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

The dashboard transforms e-commerce data into interactive business insights using Power BI.

```text
Raw E-Commerce Data
        ↓
   Power Query
(Data Cleaning & Transformation)
        ↓
   Data Model
        ↓
   DAX Measures
        ↓
 Interactive Power BI Dashboard
        ↓
Sales | Profit | Customers | Products
        ↓
Interactive Analysis & Insights


```
Users can interact with the dashboard using slicers for:

📅 Order Date
🌎 Region
👥 Customer Segment
📦 Product Category

Selecting a filter dynamically updates the dashboard visuals and KPIs.
